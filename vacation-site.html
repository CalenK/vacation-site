Holualoa Inn

I would like to talk about the Holualoa Inn located at Hawaii's Big Island, what it has to offer, what I would do there, and more.

Amenities

The Holualoa Inn has a good amount of amenities like:

  • Bed and Breakfast
  • Laundry Service
  • Free Internet
  • Free Parking
  • Indoor and Outdoor pools
  • BBQ facilities
  • Hiking
  • A Gift shop
  • A Game room
  • A Picnic area

What I would do there

Here's what I would do if or when I go there.

  • Start off the day by doing my usual morning routine before having some of the breakfast that's included
  • Take a dip in the pool for a while
  • Checking out the Gift shop
  • Trying out some of the BBQ there
  • Taking a look at the Game room and seeing what kind of games they got

Packages

Here are a few types of packages you can select for your vacation.

Sweet Romantic Suprise

Indulge in a romantic two-night stay (or three during peak season) as part of a package that includes things like silk rose petals on the bed, chocolate-dipped shortbread cookies, a 60-minute couple's massage, any accommodation of your choice, price typically depends on the season and rooms are range from $690 to $855 a night and at the time of making this it is very booked up right now but I've read online that a good time to book a stay there in general are during either May, September, and December.

Healthy Mind and Heart

Breathe and renew your senses with this relaxing, garden-to-table experience designed to align body and mind in ways such as a 60-minute private yoga lesson in your idyllic location of choice, a healthy lunch served in the ocean-view dining room featuring a mixed green salad with fruits and vegetables from the estate, choice of grilled chicken or fish and a fresh fruit smoothie (vegetarian and vegan options are available by the why), it is priced at $210 per couple, plus selected room rate, agian it seem to be pretty booked up at the time of making this but as I've already said before the best times to book a stay there seems to be during May, September, and December so yeah.
