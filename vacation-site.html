Holualoa Inn

I would like to talk about the Holualoa Inn located at Hawaii's Big Island, what it has to offer, what I would do there, and more.

Amenities

The Holualoa Inn has a good amount of amenities like:

  • Bed and Breakfast
  • Laundry Service
  • Free Internet
  • Free Parking
  • Indoor and Outdoor pools
  • BBQ facilities
  • Hiking
  • A Gift shop
  • A Game room
  • A Picnic area

What I would do there

Here's what I would do if or when I go there.

  • Start off the day by doing my usual morning routine before having some of the breakfast that's included
  • Take a dip in the pool for a while
  • Checking out the Gift shop
  • Trying out some of the BBQ there
  • Taking a look at the Game room and seeing what kind of games they got
